17Glossary and formula sheet

17.1 Glossary

TermMeaning in this project
A*best-first graph search ordered by f = g + h; optimal with an admissible, consistent h (octile distance here)
admissible heuristican estimate of remaining cost that never overestimates
ant colony optimisationmany simulated ants build paths guided by pheromone and a heuristic; pheromone rewards short paths and evaporates
auction (sequential)repeatedly assign the (robot, task) pair with the highest utility, then update the others' utilities
belief / log-oddsln(p/(1−p)); evidence adds up; used for map cells and for victim candidates
boustrophedonlawnmower coverage: parallel lanes in alternating directions
Bresenham linethe 8-connected cell chain of a straight segment; its length is the octile distance
bumpercontact sensor; driving into a cell the map wrongly called free marks it as proven solid
candidate / confirmed / rejectedstates of a sighting in the belief filter
cella 0.5 m × 0.5 m square of the navigation grid (fine grid: 0.25 m)
CNNconvolutional neural network; here a small fully convolutional heatmap detector
corner cuttinga diagonal move past an obstacle's corner; never allowed
counted twicea real person confirmed again more than 1.6 m from the first report
coverage (searched)share of reachable floor seen by a camera
dead end / tabuloop or blockage detection; back off and avoid that goal for 60 s
decoy / look-alikean object that can fool a colour camera (jacket, bag, box, beam, barrel)
differential drivetwo tracks or wheels; turns on the spot by driving them in opposite directions
dilated convolutiona convolution with gaps between its taps: a wider view for the same cost
Dijkstraoptimal shortest paths with non-negative costs; also the distance field used to pick goals
distance fieldroute distance from one cell to every cell (one Dijkstra run)
DWADynamic Window Approach: score the admissible next moves by heading, clearance, progress and turn
evidence grid / occupancy grida map of per-cell belief of being occupied
free-space assumptionplanning as if unknown cells were free (used for pattern waypoints)
frontiera searched free cell next to unsearched space
ground truththe real world state, known only to the simulator (for rendering and scoring)
Hungarian methodan optimal solver for one-to-one assignment (robots ↔ pattern regions)
informed samplingRRT* samples only inside the ellipse where a shorter route could exist
inverse sensor modelturning a measurement into evidence about cells (beam passed → free, ended → occupied)
LiDARlaser range finder: 240 distances per turn in a plane 40 cm above the floor
octile distancemax(dx, dy) + (√2 − 1)·min(dx, dy): exact cost on an empty 8-connected grid
precision / recallshare of reports that were real / share of real victims that were reported
probedrive into an unresolvable cell and let the bumper decide
ray casting / ray marchingfollowing a line through the world until it hits a surface (LiDAR, camera)
RGB-D cameracolour camera with per-pixel depth
RRT*sampling-based planner growing a tree of straight segments; asymptotically optimal with rewiring
seedthe starting number of a random generator; the same seed gives the same building and mission
SLAMsimultaneous localisation and mapping (not simulated here: positions are exact)
verify goala trip to look closely at an uncertain sighting
waypointa point on a coverage pattern, at most 1.5 m from the previous one
