8.7 One real mission, second by second

Theory is easier to believe when you watch it happen. Below is the start of a real mission: the office of Figure 3.3 (seed 3), 3 robots, both cameras and LiDAR, coordinated strategy, frontier exploration, Dijkstra. Every line is copied from the simulator's own log and from the robots' printed reasons. Nothing is invented.

What to notice
  • The layers interleave. In the same second one robot explores a frontier (coverage and strategy) while another checks a sighting (ML evidence turned into a task by the auction).
  • Sightings pull robots strongly. A likely person is worth up to 30 points against 2–9 for a typical frontier piece, so a robot will drive 17 m to check one. Finding people matters more than mapping one more corner.
  • Nothing is reported after one look. Every confirmation needed 6 frames, often from 2–3 robots. The one false candidate in this stretch (a beam) was rejected, not reported.
  • Everything is explainable. Every decision above came with a printed reason. You can watch the same reasons live in the dashboard's Robots tab and Log.
